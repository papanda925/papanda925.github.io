# Forgiveness / Trust Test

Date: 2026-10-05
Stage: test

Parent: 2026-10-05-christian-forgiveness-reconciliation-trust.md

## Candidate
Forgiveness, repair, reconciliation, trust, and boundaries should not be one switch.

## Counterexamples
1. Reconciliation must not be forced on a harmed person.
2. An apology alone does not automatically restore trust or access.
3. A boundary must not become disguised revenge or permanent punishment.
4. A community should not erase an individual's objection by declaring the matter resolved.

## Case tests
- Minor failure plus concrete repair: boundaries may be relaxed gradually.
- Repeated serious failure plus words only: avoid retaliation while retaining stronger boundaries.
- No-contact preference: no retaliation and no contact can coexist.
- Long-term improvement: reassess trust from evidence instead of freezing a past failure forever.

## Agent translation
Harm Signal → Safety / Boundary → No Retaliation → Repair Evidence → Trust Update → Access Decision → Reconciliation Option → Review

Trust Update and Access Decision must not be automatically coupled.
